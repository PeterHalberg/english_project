Lessons about labor from George Orwell "Animal farm"

1
After the rebellion, the animals transition from forced labor to collective effort. This shift transforms work from a burden into a mission, as they believe they are building a future for themselves, not for human masters.
2
For that day we all must labour,
Though we die before it break;
Cows and horses, geese and turkeys,
All must toil for freedom's sake.>
3
The animals' labor is characterized by a scrupulous, almost religious devotion. Without modern tools, they compensate with sheer willpower, seeing the farm not as a workplace, but as their last hope for sovereignty.
4
Boxer is the character, affected by this ideas the most. His labor is a good example of selflessness
5
"I will work harder" - Boxer
6
As a parallel from modern world of George Orwell the rapid industrialization of Soviet Russia, Korea or Japan is the nearest. These countries demonstrated an economic wonder.
